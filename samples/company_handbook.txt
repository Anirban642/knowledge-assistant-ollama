ACME TECH PRIVATE LIMITED - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Annual Leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month. Employees must apply for annual leave at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal. Leave of more than 5 consecutive days needs written approval from the department head.

1.2 Carry Forward
Up to 8 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward leave expires on 31 March. Unused leave beyond 8 days lapses and is not encashed.

1.3 Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave must be reported to the manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of absence.

1.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

SECTION 2: WORK FROM HOME POLICY

2.1 Hybrid Schedule
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Team leads decide the fixed work-from-home days for their team. Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, during which everyone must be reachable on chat and email.

2.2 Equipment and Internet
The company provides a laptop to every employee. Home internet is reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per month on submitting the monthly bill to the finance team.

SECTION 3: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

3.1 Business Travel
Employees traveling for business are reimbursed for economy class flights and standard hotels up to INR 4,000 per night. Claims must be submitted within 15 days of the trip with original receipts. Claims submitted after 15 days are rejected.

3.2 Meal Allowance
The meal allowance during business travel is INR 800 per day.

SECTION 4: RESIGNATION AND NOTICE PERIOD

4.1 Notice Period
Confirmed employees must serve a 60-day notice period. Employees on probation must serve a 15-day notice period. The notice period can be shortened only with written approval from both HR and the department head.

4.2 Final Settlement
The final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day, after the employee returns all company assets and completes the exit interview.
